BUKKY ANNY

Adebukola Anifowose

Humanitarian • Peacemaker • Entrepreneur • Author
Business Development Specialist • Creative Director • Founder of Bukky Group

A big heart. A busy imagination. Still figuring life out.

Hello, I’m Adebukola Anifowose, but you probably know me as Bukky Anny.

I build businesses, write my thoughts, love people and make little jokes that sometimes deserve a bigger audience. I am ambitious, playful and still navigating this thing called life.

Honestly, Bukky is just a little girl who refuses to grow up. She now has children, responsibilities and businesses, but apparently nobody informed her inner child!

My roots, my story

I am proudly Nigerian and Yoruba. My journey has taken me through Osogbo in Osun State, Egbeda Akowonjo in Lagos, Yaba in Lagos and Abuja, before bringing me to South Africa.

Each place forms part of my story. Different environments, different people and plenty of experiences have helped shape the woman I am still becoming.

I am the third child of five and the first daughter, with two older brothers, one younger sister and one younger brother.

So yes, I have experience being someone’s little sister and someone else’s big sister. A very demanding management position, if you ask me!

Business found me early

I started doing business at the age of 12.

While I was still growing up, my entrepreneurial spirit had already clocked in for work.

My love for business is out of this world. I enjoy finding possibilities, developing ideas, creating identities and working out how something can grow.

Today, that passion is part of my work as the founder of Bukky Group, a business development specialist and a creative director.

My mind is rarely quiet. One idea arrives, and before I have finished welcoming it, another three are knocking.

My family, my heart

Alongside everything I do, I am a wife and a mother. My husband and our three children are part of my personal story, beyond the businesses and public roles.

My children, Sultan, Seerat and Sofia, are my heart and my best pals.

They have a place in me that words can only begin to explain. Being their mother is one of the most meaningful parts of my life.

And with my playful nature, you may occasionally need a moment to work out who the actual children are. Don’t worry, I remember when it is time to be responsible!

Fashion, beauty and something delicious

I love everything fashion and beauty. Give me a beautiful design, a lovely outfit or an opportunity to create something special, and you have my attention.

I also love cooking and enjoy having people around me. Good food, comfortable company and laughter make me happy.

I make my little jokes, enjoy a playful moment and love seeing people smile. Life can be serious enough. Sometimes we need to eat well, laugh properly and give our worries a short break.

I stand with humans

I am a humanitarian, a peacemaker and an advocate of love and kindness.

I believe people deserve dignity, compassion and the chance to be understood. I do not believe labels or discrimination should determine how we treat one another.

Behind every introduction is a human being with a story we may know very little about.

I value peace and stay away from unnecessary trouble. Fun fact: I have never been involved in a physical fight. I would very much like to keep that record intact!

Another fact about me is that I find it difficult to speak ill of someone. I want to leave room for understanding, even when we see things differently.

A soft spot for young hearts

I love being around children and teenagers. Their curiosity, humour and way of looking at the world bring me joy.

I believe adults should be willing to help young people, rather than simply judge them.

Before criticising a child or teenager, I believe we should ask ourselves, “What am I doing to support them?”

They need people who listen, guide, encourage and show up. Sometimes, being a caring adult begins with making a young person feel safe enough to speak.

Writing is where my thoughts find their voice

I often express myself better through writing. The page gives me time to understand my feelings and find the words I mean.

Speaking those thoughts can take a little more work. I am learning to explain myself more clearly, one conversation at a time.

Sometimes my mind has written an entire book while my mouth is still deciding how to begin the introduction!

Still growing. Still glowing.

I do not have life completely figured out.

I am still learning, still discovering myself and still finding my way through change, relationships, purpose and responsibility.

I want to build meaningful businesses, create beautiful things, make people happy and contribute something good to the world.

And somewhere between all of that, I will probably make a joke, cook something delicious and come up with another business idea.

That is me.

Adebukola Anifowose. Bukky Anny. Still becoming.
